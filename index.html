How to earn silver

Every night pays silver, win or lose: more for zombies, vampires and turns, and the most for a win. Quests, achievements, the daily reward, your first win of the day and every new level pay extra. Silver buys looks only, never perks, and level unlocks stay free. Looks marked ⚰️ are found in coffins, which every level, the daily reward and the Blood Pass give you.

Looks marked ✦ cost Orb credits instead, the ones you build worlds with. They’re yours wherever you sign in to Orb, and credits buy looks only too.
